When proposing a tool, library, service, or platform, always consider and list free / OSS alternatives BEFORE recommending a paid solution.

**Why:** Variable monthly costs add up fast. Paid solutions should justify their delta over free options — not be the default.

**How to apply:** When making a stack or tool choice:
1. List 1-2 free options (preferably self-hosted OSS) with pros and cons.
2. Only then, if features missing in the free options are necessary, propose a paid one and explain the value delta.
3. If the user already pays for a service that fits the role, reuse it instead of adding a new cost.
